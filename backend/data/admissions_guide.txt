MINDSPROUT DEMO UNIVERSITY — ADMISSIONS GUIDE 2026–27
=================================================

ABOUT MINDSPROUT DEMO UNIVERSITY
Mindsprout Demo University is a leading institution offering undergraduate, postgraduate,
and doctoral programmes across Engineering, Sciences, Arts, Commerce, and Management.
Established in 1991, the university is NAAC A+ accredited and ranked among the top 50
universities in India by NIRF 2026.

PROGRAMMES OFFERED
Undergraduate (UG):
  - B.Tech (CSE, ECE, Mechanical, Civil, AI & ML)
  - B.Sc (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biotechnology)
  - B.Com (General, Hons, Financial Markets)
  - BBA (Marketing, Finance, Human Resources)
  - BA (English, Psychology, Sociology, Economics)

Postgraduate (PG):
  - M.Tech (CSE, ECE, Data Science)
  - MBA (2-year full time, specialisations: Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations)
  - M.Sc (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Data Science)
  - MCA (Master of Computer Applications)
  - MA (English, Economics, Psychology)

Doctoral:
  - Ph.D in all major departments (full-time and part-time)

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
B.Tech / B.Sc / BBA / B.Com / BA:
  - Minimum 50% in Class 12 from a recognised board
  - For B.Tech: PCM mandatory; minimum 55% in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics
  - Age limit: 17–23 years as on 1 July of admission year

M.Tech:
  - B.Tech / B.E. with minimum 60% aggregate
  - Valid GATE score preferred (mandatory for scholarship consideration)

MBA:
  - Any bachelor's degree with minimum 50%
  - CAT / MAT / XAT score required
  - Work experience preferred but not mandatory

Ph.D:
  - Master's degree with minimum 55% in relevant discipline
  - Entrance test (NUET) or valid NET / JRF score

ADMISSION PROCESS
Step 1 – Fill the online application at admissions.northfield.edu.in
Step 2 – Upload documents: mark sheets, ID proof, passport photo
Step 3 – Pay application fee: Rs 1,000 (UG/PG) | Rs 1,500 (Ph.D)
Step 4 – Appear for merit list / entrance test (NUET) if applicable
Step 5 – Counselling and seat allotment
Step 6 – Pay first-semester fee to confirm admission
Step 7 – Report for document verification on the date specified in offer letter
