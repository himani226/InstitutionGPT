MINDSPROUT DEMO UNIVERSITY — FACULTY HANDBOOK 2026–27
=================================================

SECTION 1: FACULTY CADRE & PAY SCALES
Designation            Pay Scale (7th CPC)     Minimum Experience
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Assistant Professor     Rs 57,700 – 98,000      Ph.D or NET qualified
Associate Professor     Rs 1,31,400 – 2,17,100  8 years total teaching
Professor               Rs 1,44,200 – 2,18,200  10 years post-PhD
Distinguished Prof      Rs 1,82,200+            Special appointment
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Allowances: HRA (24% for city), TA/DA as per university norms, medical allowance
Gratuity: as per Gratuity Act; provident fund: 12% employer contribution

SECTION 2: TEACHING LOAD
Assistant Professor:  14 hours/week (contact hours)
Associate Professor:  12 hours/week
Professor:            10 hours/week
All faculty must hold minimum 2 student consultation hours per week (posted outside office)

SECTION 3: RESEARCH & PUBLICATION POLICY
Minimum annual targets (Assistant Professor):
  - 1 journal paper (Scopus/SCI indexed) per year
  - Attend / present at minimum 1 national conference
  - Apply for minimum 1 funded project in 2 years

Research incentives:
  - SCI/Scopus Q1 paper:   Rs 25,000 cash award
  - SCI/Scopus Q2 paper:   Rs 15,000 cash award
  - Patent granted:        Rs 50,000 + 50% revenue sharing
  - National conference:   Full TA/DA + Rs 5,000
  - International conf:    Full TA/DA + Rs 15,000 + 5 duty leave days
  - Funded project (PI):   10% overhead to personal research account

Research labs available:
  - AI & ML Lab (GPU cluster: 4× A100 + 8× RTX 4090)
  - IoT & Embedded Systems Lab
  - VLSI Design Lab
  - Materials Science Lab
  - Bioinformatics & Genomics Lab
  - Business Analytics & Finance Lab

SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY
Earned leave:       30 days per year (max 60 days accumulation)
Casual leave:       12 days per year (max 3 consecutive days)
Medical leave:      15 days per year (certificate required for >2 days)
Maternity leave:    180 days (as per Maternity Benefit Act 2017)
Paternity leave:    15 days
Study leave:        Up to 2 years for PhD (after 5 years service, once in career)
Academic duty leave: As required (conference/workshop/exam duty)

SECTION 5: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
FDP (Faculty Development Programme): each faculty must attend minimum 1 FDP per year
Sponsored: university pays registration + TA/DA for approved programmes
Online certifications (Coursera/NPTEL): reimbursed up to Rs 5,000/year
Sabbatical leave: 1 year at full pay after 7 years (for research/industry attachment)

SECTION 6: ACADEMIC RESPONSIBILITIES
- Upload course materials to LMS (Moodle) before semester start
- Upload attendance within 24 hours of each class on ERP portal
- Mid-semester marks: submit within 3 days of test completion
- End-semester marks: submit within 48 hours of last exam
- Project evaluations: publish schedule at least 7 days in advance
- Syllabus completion certificate: submit to HOD at semester end

SECTION 7: FACULTY RESEARCH PORTAL
Access: research.mindsprout.edu.in | Login: faculty ID + NF password
Features: grant management, publication tracker, lab booking, collaboration network
Funded projects database (active): PI can view, co-PI can request access
