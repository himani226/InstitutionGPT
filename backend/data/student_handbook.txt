MINDSPROUT DEMO UNIVERSITY — STUDENT HANDBOOK 2026–27
=================================================

SECTION 1: CODE OF CONDUCT
Students must maintain dignity and integrity at all times on and off campus.
Ragging in any form — physical, verbal, online — is strictly prohibited and
punishable under UGC Anti-Ragging Regulations 2009. First offence: suspension.
Repeat offence: expulsion and police complaint.

Dress Code:
  - Formal attire Monday to Friday (no torn/distressed clothing, no shorts)
  - College ID card must be worn visibly at all times within campus
  - Casuals permitted Saturday only

Mobile phones:
  - Permitted in campus but strictly prohibited inside classrooms and labs unless
    instructed by faculty for academic purposes

SECTION 2: LIBRARY RULES
Working hours: Mon–Sat 8:00 AM – 9:00 PM | Sun 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Book issue limits:
  - UG students:    4 books, 14 days
  - PG students:    6 books, 21 days
  - Ph.D scholars:  8 books, 30 days
  - Faculty:       10 books, 60 days
Fine for late return: Rs 2 per book per day
Reference books and journals cannot be issued (reading room only)
E-library access (IEEE, Scopus, JSTOR, Springer): my.minsprout.edu.in/elibrary
Login: student ID | Password: NF + date of birth (DDMMYYYY)

SECTION 3: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL
Step 1 – Approach the concerned faculty/HOD informally within 5 working days
Step 2 – Submit written complaint to Department Grievance Committee (DGC)
Step 3 – Escalate to University Grievance Cell (UGC) if unresolved in 10 days
Step 4 – Vice Chancellor's office for final appeal

Anti-sexual harassment committee (ICC): icc@mindsprout.edu.in
Student welfare officer: Dr Priya Menon, Room 203, Admin Block

SECTION 4: HOSTEL REGULATIONS
Check-in / check-out: must be logged in hostel register
Guest policy: visitors allowed in visitor lounge only (not in rooms)
Curfew: 10:00 PM on weekdays | 11:00 PM on weekends
Overnight outing: written consent from parent/guardian required (Form H-7)
Cooking in rooms: strictly prohibited (fire hazard)
Alcohol and tobacco: banned on entire campus including hostels
Violation penalty: warning → fine (Rs 2,000) → hostel expulsion

SECTION 5: EXAM REGULATIONS
- Students must carry valid college ID and hall ticket to exam hall
- Entry not permitted 15 minutes after exam start
- Exit not permitted in first 60 minutes
- Use of unfair means (copying, mobile phones, chits): cancel paper + debarment
- Malpractice in more than one exam: semester cancellation
- Re-evaluation of answer scripts: apply within 7 days of result | Fee Rs 300/paper
- Photocopy of answer script: Rs 500 per paper
