DOCTORAL PROGRAMME
Ph.D (full-time):  Rs 20,000 per semester (tuition) + Rs 5,000 development fee
Ph.D (part-time):  Rs 15,000 per semester (tuition) + Rs 5,000 development fee

ONE-TIME FEES (paid at admission only)
Registration fee:            Rs 2,000
Caution deposit (refundable): Rs 5,000
Library deposit (refundable): Rs 2,000
Alumni membership:            Rs 1,000

HOSTEL FEES (per year)
Double occupancy room:  Rs 60,000 (includes food)
Triple occupancy room:  Rs 50,000 (includes food)
Single room (limited):  Rs 90,000 (includes food)
Mess-only (day scholars): Rs 30,000/year

TRANSPORT FEES (per year, optional)
Within 10 km:   Rs 12,000
10–20 km:       Rs 18,000
20–35 km:       Rs 24,000

PAYMENT MODES
- Online portal: fees.northfield.edu.in (Net banking, UPI, Debit/Credit card)
- DD in favour of "Northfield University" payable at Ludhiana
- No cash accepted above Rs 2,000 (as per university policy)

FEE DUE DATES
Odd semester (July–Dec):  Fee due by 15 July each year
Even semester (Jan–Jun):  Fee due by 10 January each year
Late fee penalty:         Rs 50 per day after due date (max 30 days)
After 30 days:            Enrolment suspended until clearance

SCHOLARSHIPS AND FEE CONCESSIONS
Merit scholarship (>85% in qualifying exam):      25% tuition waiver
Sports quota scholarship:                         20% tuition waiver
Defence/ex-servicemen ward:                       15% tuition waiver
SC/ST students:                                   Full fee waiver (state govt scheme)
EWS category:                                     10% tuition waiver
Northfield alumni sibling:                        10% tuition waiver
Research assistantship (Ph.D full-time):          Full tuition + Rs 15,000/month stipend

EDUCATION LOAN ASSISTANCE
Northfield has MoUs with SBI, PNB, and Canara Bank.
Loans up to Rs 20 lakh (UG) and Rs 40 lakh (PG) available.
Contact finance office for bank liaison support: finance@northfield.edu.in
