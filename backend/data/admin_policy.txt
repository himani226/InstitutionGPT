NORTHFIELD UNIVERSITY — ADMINISTRATIVE POLICIES 2026–27
========================================================

SECTION 1: RECORDS & CERTIFICATES
Official certificates issued by Registrar's office, Room 105, Admin Block
Processing time and fees:
  Document                         Time       Fee
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Bonafide certificate             Same day   Free
  Migration certificate            3 days     Rs 200
  Character certificate            2 days     Rs 100
  Provisional degree certificate   7 days     Rs 500
  Official transcript              5 days     Rs 300/copy
  Degree certificate (convocation) On date    Free
  Duplicate degree certificate     15 days    Rs 1,000
  Medium of instruction cert.      3 days     Rs 200
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────

Urgent processing (2× fee): available for bonafide, character, transcripts only
Apply online: erp.northfield.edu.in → My Applications → Certificate Request
Physical collection: window 3, Registrar's office, 10 AM – 3 PM weekdays

SECTION 3: ANTI-PLAGIARISM POLICY
All UG major projects and PG dissertations must pass Turnitin check:
  Acceptable similarity index: below 15% overall, below 5% from any single source
  Submission: upload via lms.northfield.edu.in (faculty will initiate check)
  Plagiarism penalty:
    First offence (15–30%):   revision and resubmission
    Second offence (>30%):    fail the paper + academic warning letter
    Third offence / deliberate: expulsion from programme

SECTION 4: TRANSPORT & PARKING
Bus routes: 12 routes covering major areas of the city and suburbs
Route schedule: transport.northfield.edu.in | Updated each semester
Bus pass: Rs 1,500/year (must carry pass + ID; spot check by conductors)
Parking:
  Faculty/Staff: Parking Zone A (near Admin block) — sticker required from security
  Students:      Parking Zone C (rear campus) — two-wheelers only
  No vehicle entry for students without valid parking permit (fine Rs 500)

SECTION 5: PROCUREMENT & VENDOR POLICY
All purchases above Rs 10,000 require three quotations
Above Rs 1 lakh: purchase committee approval required
Above Rs 5 lakh: tender process (published on official website)
Lab equipment indenting: submit to HOD by 31 October for next year's budget
Travel reimbursement: submit bills within 10 days of return (Form F-12 on ERP)

SECTION 6: DATA PRIVACY
Student data collected: name, contact, academic records, biometric (attendance)
Data not shared with third parties without consent
FERPA-equivalent protections apply under university by-laws
Data retention: academic records permanent; personal data 5 years post-graduation
Requests for own data: file RTI-equivalent form at registrar's office
